I'm Sorry
(Even Though I Never Said That)

A Very Sincere — and Very Confused — Apology

🚫

I did NEVER call you ugly.
I did NEVER call you dumb.

I would sooner call the sun dim. I would sooner call chocolate boring.
(Neither of those things are possible, for the record.)

💗

But Here's What I Am Truly Sorry For

I'm sorry that something I said — or did, or didn't say, or maybe the way I sneezed — made you feel that way, even for a single second. You should never feel anything other than the most spectacular, brilliant person in the room. Because you are. Every single room. Always.

Your feelings matter to me more than being right. And I'd apologize a hundred times over just to see you smile.

✨

Allow Me To Formally Set The Record Straight

  • 🌹 You are devastatingly beautiful. Like, genuinely unfair. People probably walk into walls.
  • 🌷 You are sharp, clever, and perceptive — you notice things most people miss entirely, and that is a rare and wonderful thing.
  • 🌸 You are my favourite notification. Not the app. You. Specifically you.
  • 🍀 When you laugh at something I say, I feel like I won something. You are the prize, and the judge, and the whole competition.
  • 💐 The world is genuinely a more interesting place because you exist in it. I mean that with my whole chest.
